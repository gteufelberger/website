Having gotten a bit sick during the weekend I had the "pleasure" of staying at home all day. Trying to figure out what to do I was browsing my Steam library when I discovered [Journey](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journey_(2012_video_game)>) in my shared library.

Checking [HowLongToBeat](https://howlongtobeat.com/game/4867) I saw it's only about two hours on average, perfect for a Saturday afternoon.
And so I gave it a shot and I have to say the game definitely deserves it's high ratings :D

The game was simple but intuitive with visual clues guiding the player without the need for any hints, tooltips, or [waymarkers](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2026/sep/23/why-are-gamers-ripping-into-the-new-wolverine-game).

The visual were also stunning and the story, despite there not being a single spoken word, was very much captivating.
Overall the perfect game for a chill afternoon.

If you want to check it out yourself, here's the [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/638230/journey/).

That's all :)
